# Euclidean geometry — the IEB's ACCEPTABLE REASONS (SAGs 2026, Appendix G)

Extracted 2026-08-23 from `27. MATHEMATICS SAGs 2026 (Updated August 2025).pdf`, pages 29–32.
**Every reason in an Exam Focus Euclidean memo uses one of these short forms, verbatim** —
the learner must see the exact wording the marker accepts. Where two forms are listed,
use the first unless the question's wording makes the other clearer.

## Lines
| Statement | Acceptable reason |
|---|---|
| Adjacent angles on a straight line are supplementary | `∠s on a str line` |
| If adjacent angles are supplementary, the outer arms form a straight line | `adj ∠s supp` |
| Adjacent angles in a revolution add to 360° | `∠s round a pt` OR `∠s in a rev` |
| Vertically opposite angles are equal | `vert opp ∠s =` |
| If AB ∥ CD, alternate angles equal | `alt ∠s; AB ∥ CD` |
| If AB ∥ CD, corresponding angles equal | `corresp ∠s; AB ∥ CD` |
| If AB ∥ CD, co-interior angles supplementary | `co-int ∠s; AB ∥ CD` |
| Alternate angles equal ⇒ lines parallel | `alt ∠s =` |
| Corresponding angles equal ⇒ lines parallel | `corresp ∠s =` |
| Co-interior angles supplementary ⇒ lines parallel | `co-int ∠s supp` |

## Triangles
| Statement | Acceptable reason |
|---|---|
| Interior angles of a triangle add to 180° | `sum of ∠s in Δ` OR `int ∠s Δ` |
| Exterior angle of a triangle = sum of interior opposite angles | `ext ∠ of Δ` |
| Angles opposite equal sides (isosceles) are equal | `∠s opp equal sides` |
| Sides opposite equal angles are equal | `sides opp equal ∠s` |
| Pythagoras | `Pythagoras` |
| Converse Pythagoras | `converse Pythagoras` |
| Congruency | `SSS` · `SAS` (or `S∠S`) · `AAS` (or `∠∠S`) · `RHS` (or `90°HS`) |

## Circles
| Statement | Acceptable reason |
|---|---|
| Tangent ⊥ radius/diameter at the point of contact | `tan ⊥ radius` / `tan ⊥ diameter` |
| Line ⊥ radius at the circle ⇒ tangent | `line ⊥ radius` OR `converse tan ⊥ radius` |
| Line from centre to midpoint of chord is ⊥ chord | `line from centre to midpt of chord` |
| Line from centre ⊥ chord bisects the chord | `line from centre ⊥ to chord` |
| Perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the centre | `perp bisector of chord` |
| Angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference (same arc) | `∠ at centre = 2 × ∠ at circumference` |
| Angle in a semicircle is 90° | `∠s in semi-circle` OR `diameter subtends right angle` |
| Chord subtends 90° at circumference ⇒ chord is a diameter | `chord subtends 90°` OR `converse ∠s in semi-circle` |
| Angles on the same side of a chord are equal | `∠s in the same seg` |
| Line segment subtends equal angles at two points on the same side ⇒ concyclic | `line subtends equal ∠s` OR `converse ∠s in the same seg` |
| Equal chords subtend equal angles (circumference or centre) | `equal chords; equal ∠s` |
| Opposite angles of a cyclic quad are supplementary | `opp ∠s of cyclic quad` |
| Opposite angles supplementary ⇒ cyclic | `opp ∠s quad supp` OR `converse opp ∠s of cyclic quad` |
| Exterior angle of a cyclic quad = interior opposite angle | `ext ∠ of cyclic quad` |
| Exterior angle = interior opposite ⇒ cyclic | `ext ∠ = int opp ∠` OR `converse ext ∠ of cyclic quad` |
| Two tangents from the same external point are equal | `tans from common pt` OR `tans from same pt` |
| Tangent–chord angle = angle in the alternate segment | `tan chord theorem` |
| Line through chord end-point making the alternate-segment angle ⇒ tangent | `converse tan chord theorem` OR `∠ between line and chord` |

## Quadrilaterals
| Statement | Acceptable reason |
|---|---|
| Interior angles of a quadrilateral add to 360° | `sum of ∠s in quad` |
| Opposite sides of a parallelogram are parallel / equal | `opp sides of ∥m` |
| Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal | `opp ∠s of ∥m` |
| Diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other | `diag of ∥m` |
| Diagonals of a rhombus bisect at right angles / bisect the angles | `diags of rhombus` |
| Diagonals of a rectangle are equal | `diags of rect` |
| Diagonals of a kite intersect at right angles | `diags of kite` |

## Grade 11 scope (SAGs p46–47 + the paper bank)
- **Examinable bookwork proofs (acute case only), converses never:** line from centre ⊥ chord
  bisects it · ∠ at centre = 2 × ∠ at circumference · opposite ∠s of a cyclic quad · tan–chord.
- **Use as results (never proved):** ∠ in semicircle · ext ∠ of cyclic quad · ∠s in same segment ·
  tans from common pt · tan ⊥ radius.
- **Diagrams for proofs are always given.** Riders emphasise proof: "prove x = 20°", "prove D̂ = 2x + y",
  "prove AB ∥ CD", "prove ABCD is a cyclic quad", "name 4 other angles equal to x".
- **NO concurrency, NO proof by contradiction.**
- The angle NOTATION the exam uses: `Ô₁`, `∠ABC`, `B̂` — a subscript when a vertex carries more
  than one angle.

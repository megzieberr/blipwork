Dice · General Trig pool · verification

js/quests/dice-gtrig.js — 67 entries, 200 seeded rolls each. Part 0 proves the pool's shape and that its KEEP list plus its DROPPED list account for every one of the chapter's 93 audited skills, so nothing can go missing silently. Part 1 is the per-entry integrity pass. Half this chapter is steps — an ordered chain of sub-inputs with none of q.options / q.expected / q.yes at question level — so the generic dice checks RECURSE into q.steps and dispatch on all eight sub-kinds (mc, mcmulti, calc, tokenpad, tapcross, doubletick, sketchfill, tapside). The shape validation is lifted from verify-gtrig.html §9 and widened; on top of it every sub-step is put through checkStep() — the app's own marking brain — with its right answer and with a wrong one. Part 2 is graph honesty: every rolled figure, question-level and step-level, back through verifyQuadTri / verifyTrig / verifyTriangle. Part 3 is the dice layer: determinism, salted retry, dealing, coverage and resume.

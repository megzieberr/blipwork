f10 — the round's shape, words and rulings, card by card

Every f10 card: a hint, an answer and a worked solution ending on the refrain "Deposits and the loan go IN. Withdrawals and repayments come OUT." with its closing line by kind: a loan "Tidy: payments positive on the right.", saving "Tidy: deposits plus, the withdrawal minus, A on the left." (review 1). Every "=" glues to the term before it inside that term's unit (a bare "=" between units does not parse), no car is ever ^1, "half‑yearly" keeps its non-breaking hyphen, and a deposit on the day of A draws its own leader down beside the "?" ring. Nothing says lumpsum, lump sum, accumulated amount, LHS or RHS; no HTML entity, no plain space inside an amount, no money amount outside its unbreakable unit; every equation parses term by term. On a picture the total is "?" with its role letter (or, on the interest card, the known loan with its P), the meeting day is the last T, and a saving picture draws exactly one withdrawal "−R…". The shape: the jug cards roll loans and saving, jugWrong slips each of the four kinds (a repayment added, the loan subtracted, the withdrawal added, a deposit subtracted), a repayment sometimes stands on the meeting day and a deposit sometimes on the day of A, the recall card deals all five items, the interest card offers a negative decoy, signOnTimeline names no amount in words.
